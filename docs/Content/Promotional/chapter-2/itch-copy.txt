CHAPTER 2 - ENERGY RESEARCH
The great door was never meant to open for you. Beyond it, the plant's research
levels keep their own hours: an intake theatre with its film on a loop, a
screening hall still waiting on its morning queue, the suits hung where the
staff left them. Lower down, the work they were doing is still going on
without them.

- Forty-two new areas, from the clean front offices to the flooded test halls
  and what grew in Containment.
- Steel grilles you can be seen through, time-locked cages, standing water you
  cannot see the bottom of.
- A new score for the laboratories and the clinic, and rooms that sound like
  what they are made of.
- Hide where you can. Lockers hold now, until something hears you knock.

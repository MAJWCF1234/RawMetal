BUILD IT LIKE A BLUEPRINT
No Unity, Blender, scripting, or level-design vocabulary required.
Depthworks turns an ordinary floor plan into the 3D game space. Work the same way you would sketch an industrial building: draw rooms, place equipment, mark where people go, then choose finishes.
1Draw the building

Use ROOM and WALL like a simple paint program. The blueprint quietly expands across internal map boundaries for you.

2Place equipment

Open EQUIPMENT and click or drag pumps, cabinets, lamps, machinery, clutter, and other real game assets onto the plan.

3Place people

PEOPLE drops simple scale dummies for workers, security, scientists, NPCs, creatures, or the player start. Turn them to show facing.

4Watch it become 3D

LIVE PLAN + 3D updates from the same drawing. WALK lets you inspect the building at human scale without exporting or compiling.

Useful building-designer tools: switch floors from the ribbon over the plan, use CEILING / RCP for overhead equipment, BUILDING SECTION for vertical placement, FINISHES to paint real game textures, DIMENSION for saved drawing dimensions, REPEAT / ARRAY on selected equipment for rack, light, cabinet, or machine rows, and WALKABILITY when you want the editor to show whether the drawn floor is actually connected. Raw coordinates and 24 × 24 m plan areas are optional advanced details.

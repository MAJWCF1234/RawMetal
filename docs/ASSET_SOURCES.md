# Asset source provenance

This file centralizes source/provenance notes that were previously scattered through `src/assets/**/SOURCES.md` and related notes.

Availability note, October 10, 2026: the user has taken the external source drive
away temporarily. Treat `O:/retro official` as offline; project copies and embedded
resources remain the working asset set. See [project notes](PROJECT_NOTES.md).

## First-person arms
Source archive: `psx-first-person-arms-free-game-assets.zip`.
The supplied `arms_rig.glb` / `arms_rig.fbx` expose 18 clips including grab, guard, jab, knife, relax and rest motions. RawMetal uses the supplied rig data for firearm hand poses and unarmed animation.

## Audio
Source library: `O:/retro official`.
RawMetal audio was copied or converted from the purchased/source library while leaving originals unchanged. Runtime resources include shotgun fire/pump, metal and concrete footsteps, jump/landing, pickup/hurt, creature calls/attacks/deaths, machine ambience, door, melee, clutter impacts, lift motor/creak/snap/crash, and reactor/music loops.

Surface-lift/reactor sources include Rust & Blood v1.1d, ROT v1.9, and Echoes v2.1 assets. Runtime WAV effects are 44.1 kHz PCM16; MP3 music is decoded at runtime.

## Physical clutter
Primary source: `PSX Bunkers v1.8.8.zip` under `O:/retro official`.
Runtime clutter includes trash, MRE, power supply, PCB and floppy-disc meshes/textures. The glass bottle remains from the purchased Street Furniture asset set.

## Environment

## Facility
Purchased sources include Modular Retro FPS Kit v1.5 and PSX Bunkers v1.8.8. RawMetal uses supplied columns, vents, ceiling lamps, generators, shelving, wall cabinets and computer/control-terminal assets. Model UVs and per-face material assignments are retained.

## Cleanup squad

User supplied `Cleanupteam.zip` from Downloads on October 11, 2026. Originals
are retained under `source-assets/cleanup-team/`; SHA256 and byte counts were
verified before removing the six specifically requested Downloads originals.
`diagnostics/kill-squad-20261011/asset-transfer.json` records the transfer.

The archive's `MercenariesPack/final.blend` supplies Assault, Engineer and
Sharpshooter, Heavy, Medic and Rusher meshes. Their corresponding body/head PNGs are copied unchanged.
Runtime derivatives are under `src/assets/actors/cleanup/`, generated by
`tools/ImportCleanupActor.py`. Source UVs/materials are preserved, with engine
axis conversion and a 1.6 m standing height. Runtime arm calibration and six
baked vertex animation clips are newly authored here; the source contained no
combat rig or locomotion/death clips. They are prototype motion, not supplied
skeletal animations.

Investigator, Limitator and the three separately supplied texture files remain
safely retained as originals. They are not embedded in this squad pass. The
Limitator is reserved for sparse future use; ordinary squads use cleanup members.

## Engineer military drone

The supplied `Downloads/military-drone-low-poly/source/Drone1.zip` originals are
retained under `source-assets/military-drone/`. `tools/ImportMilitaryDrone.py`
preserves UVs and textures and bakes the supplied Idle and Walk animations.
Runtime derivatives are embedded from `src/assets/actors/drone/`, normalized
to a 0.90 m span. Deployment, supervision and repair use shared engine behavior.

## Medic treatment and sidearm

The reconnected library is mounted at `O:/retro official`. Medic treatment uses
`Animations/Universal Animation Library 2[Standard]/Unity/UAL2_Standard.fbx`,
the `Chest_Open` action. The library's supplied License.txt declares CC0 1.0.
Source and license are retained in `source-assets/medic-motion/`; its grounded
retarget bake and hand anchors come from `tools/BakeMedicTreatment.py`.
`Weapons & Items/RetroWeaponPack_V1/.../Guns/Pistol_01` supplies the Medic's
handgun model and original albedo, retained in `source-assets/medic-handgun/`.
`tools/ImportMedicHandgun.py` converts its axes with UVs and physical scale intact.

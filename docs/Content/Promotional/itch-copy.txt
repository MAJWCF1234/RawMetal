DESCRIPTION
You work the night shift at the Depthworks plant. Tonight the lift takes you
somewhere your badge doesn't open.

DEPTHWORKS is a first-person stealth and survival game. Creep, hide, listen,
and decide how much of the dark you can afford to cross.

- Keep to the dark. Light, distance and movement give you away. Lockers,
  shadows and patience keep you breathing.
- Listen. Footsteps, radios, machinery. The plant tells you more than any map.
- Bring it back. Switch halls, turbine floors, flooded galleries. Every
  district you wake opens the next, and lights you up for whatever else is
  down there.
- One file, nothing to install. A low-res, late-90s look on its own engine and
  renderer, written from scratch.

CONTROLS
WASD move, mouse look, Shift sprint, Space jump, C / Ctrl crouch, E use,
F flashlight, Q subdue, R reload, I inventory, Esc menu.

NEEDS
Windows 10/11 x64, a GPU with a Vulkan 1.0 driver, the Microsoft Visual C++
x64 Redistributable. VR (experimental): SteamVR with Quest, Index or Vive.

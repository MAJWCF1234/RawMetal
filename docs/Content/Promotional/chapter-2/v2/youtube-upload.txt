Behind the great door, the work went on without them. So did R-117.
DEPTHWORKS Chapter 2: Energy Research is coming. Chapter 1 is free now: https://majwcf1234.itch.io/depthworks

The research levels under the plant kept their own hours after the staff
stopped keeping them. The welcome film is still running. The mortuary is still
full. Containment kept its specimens, mostly. One of them is not in its cell.
It moves low, takes a man's head, stays down to feed, and comes back for the
rest. Some of the equipment is awake too, and only the current kills it.

DEPTHWORKS is a first-person stealth and survival game set in a power plant on
its worst night. Chapter 1 is complete and free. Chapter 2 is coming.

Built on RawMetal, a custom engine and renderer written from scratch.
All footage is in-engine, captured from the running simulation: no UI, no
pre-rendered animation. Music and sound design from licensed libraries.

Windows 10/11 (x64), Vulkan 1.0 GPU. Experimental VR (SteamVR).

Play Chapter 1 free:  https://majwcf1234.itch.io/depthworks
Release notes:        https://github.com/MAJWCF1234/RawMetal/releases

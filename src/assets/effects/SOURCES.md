# Weapon effects

`muzzle-flash.png` was copied from `O:/retro official/Weapons & Items/RetroWeaponPack_V1/Assets/RetroWeaponsPack/FX/Textures/MuzzleFlash.png`. The original is unchanged. The renderer applies warm additive color, time-varying size, rotation and intensity over a 100 ms burst. It uses the weapon's actual muzzle position and follows recoil, roll and lowering.

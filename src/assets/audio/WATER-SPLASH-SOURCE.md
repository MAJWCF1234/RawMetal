Gunshot water-impact one-shot from the purchased Horror SFX Complete pack
(YourPalRob), Liquids folder:

- `Liquid_plash poor.ogg` — the strike
- `Splash_thrown object_1.ogg` — the wet body that follows under the gun

Mixed to about 0.58 s of mono 44.1 kHz PCM16, high-passed at 180 Hz and
low-passed at 6.5 kHz, peak-normalised. The thrown splash enters at 45 ms so
the wet cue stays audible under a shotgun tail. Embedded resource 248 is the
RMS1 encode of `water-splash.wav`. The pack files are unchanged.

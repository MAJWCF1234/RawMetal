Neural texture round 4 (2026-10-30)
Screened the 290 opaque colour textures imported from the purchased packs since
round 3 (59 with alpha and 9 under 16 px were left alone) at x2.
Round-3 bar: PSNR >= 32 and SSIM >= 0.84, packet at least 20% smaller than the
lossless packed PNG; the byte floor drops from 8 KB to 4 KB because these
textures are mostly 128-256 px. 67 passed.
Side-by-side sheets were inspected at native resolution. Twelve were rejected
where the softening shows: clock numerals, first-aid and box labels, breaker
latch, switch label, plank and cabinet wood grain, TV frame, couch fabric and
the three paintings. 55 ship as x2 neural textures, saving 1,536,222 packed
bytes. Their .ntx files sit beside the gitignored library sources.

DEVLOG / ANNOUNCEMENT
The great door at the bottom of Chapter 1 is still shut. Behind it, the plant's
research levels are keeping their own hours. The welcome film in the intake
theatre is still running for a shift that never came in. The mortuary tables in
Medical Deep are still occupied. The chambers in the Energy Test Labs are still
lit, and nobody is reading the instruments.

Containment kept the specimens. Mostly.

R-117 is an animal that was given a number and a cell. It is not in its cell.
It moves low, with its feet set down where it means them, and when it comes it
comes all at once. It takes a man's head and stays down on him to feed, and while
it feeds it is busy. That is your time. It does not stay busy, and it does not
stay in one room. It comes back.

Some of the equipment down there is awake as well. It sleeps until something
wakes it, and only the plant's own current puts it down.

Chapter 2 is coming. Chapter 1 is free now.

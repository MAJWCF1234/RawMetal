Leon (a player character)
-------------------------
Leon's model is based on "Boykisser Custom Low Poly Avatar (Scratch Model)"
by NEGATIVEMARE (https://skfb.ly/pO6ZD), licensed under CC BY 4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: the model's three meshes were joined and reduced, its rig was mapped
onto the game's skeleton, its materials were reduced to flat colours, and it is
animated with the game's own character animations.

The author did not make, review or endorse DepthWorks.

Skinbot leg rig: the approach of stepping legs solved from foot targets follows
minecraft-spider by TheCymaera, https://github.com/TheCymaera/minecraft-spider
(its README allows commercial and non-commercial use of the source). The code here is our own.

# The Descent: Chapters 1B to 4

This plan carries the main campaign from CARGO / DEEP JUNCTION (map 31,
manifest 18) to the end of the Dark Below (manifest 136). It keeps the
[[campaign-system-overview-20261010]] manifest order and names, and the lore
already in the game. Chapter 4 names stay tonal placeholders where the
overview says so.

## Story spine

### What is down there

- **Resonance.** Depthworks found an energy seam deep under the plant. Staff
  call it *resonance*; the reactor, the labs and the mine all exist to measure
  it, bottle it and sell it as power. Specimens and entities born from it carry
  `R-` numbers (R-03 is already in the reactor core hall).
- **Its rules.** Resonance behaves consistently, so the player can learn it:
  - It pools where metal is wet and still. Standing water near live gear hums
    before it bites.
  - It hates light that moves. Torches and muzzle flashes make the small things
    scatter; the big things come toward them.
  - It keeps what it touches. Machines restart on their own; doors remember
    who opened them; transformed workers keep their habits.
  - Its fields show as a shimmer and a low hum (hazard kind Anomaly) before they
    burn.
- **Exposure.** Workers absorb it. Medical Screening was never a clinic for
  injuries: it graded staff by exposure tier and moved high readers into
  observation. Depthworks knew, and kept the shifts running.

### The friend (handle LAB 4)

The prologue ends on "NO SIGNAL FROM LAB 4". Her trail is the campaign's
breadcrumb, paid off in steps, never in one cut-scene:

1. **Load Control (manifest 24).** Restoring power wakes the mail relay. One
   queued message from LAB 4, stamped after the crash: "if you're alive don't
   come down to the labs. go up." There is no up.
2. **Research Administration (47).** Her personnel card: Energy Diagnostics
   analyst, Lab 4, "EXPOSURE TIER 2 / SCREENING DUE".
3. **Medical Screening (48), the clinic front.** Her intake line on the
   triage board: "LAB 4 / HELD FOR OBSERVATION / WARD C". Ward C is sealed.
4. **Lab 4, inside Energy Diagnostics (53).** Her workstation, the terminal the
   prologue's chat came from. Her drafts to the player, the last unsent. She
   was escorted to Medical after the surge. The medical override disk lies on
   the escort's body.
5. **The medical wing (Ward C and beyond).** The player returns to the clinic
   with the disk. The blackout, the patients, the morgue. Her bed is empty, her
   band cut. "SUBJECT LEFT WARD UNASSISTED. DOORS OPENED FROM INSIDE."
6. **Quarantine and Isolation (66-67).** Her handwriting on the bunker walls
   and terminals starts guiding the player: "follow the lamps." She is changing
   and she knows it.
7. **The mines (77-98).** Her badge at the Abandoned Heading. Lamps left lit
   along a survey drift no one else used.
8. **The Dark Below (99-136).** She lives among the transformed in the scrap
   city, still herself enough to talk through their terminals. At the Dark
   Heart she goes on through the gate alone and sends the player up. The last
   line is hers.

### The Cleanup Force

- Chapter 1B: the Force sweeps the power plant and the perimeter. Its
  checkpoints are the main stealth spaces (Security Perimeter, Checkpoint
  Delta).
- Chapter 2: it is losing. Squads hold the containment sectors and are being
  taken. The player sees them as colleagues who were sent in as badly briefed
  as anyone.
- Chapter 3: a last team holds the drill and the mine head. It is dug in, not
  hunting.
- Chapter 4: the Force raids the scrap city to erase it. The Limitator
  (kept out of ordinary squads until now) leads the last assault at the Deep
  Gate.

### The transformed

- In the plant and labs they are the danger: patients, specimens and workers
  the energy kept.
- In the Dark Below the aware ones made a home of what the plant threw away.
  Waste Handling and Salvage Dispatch sent scrap down the chasm; it became
  their city. They are mostly peaceful. Shooting them is possible and it is
  remembered by their terminals. The fauna outside the walls is not peaceful.

## Structure (not a corridor)

The route is a chain of districts. Each district is a hub with spokes, and
progress usually needs a trip out and a trip back:

- A hub always shows its locked doors early, with the reason written on them.
- Each spoke ends in a change of state (power, pressure, an override disk) that
  opens something back at the hub, or a shortcut that loops to it.
- Stitched groups of chunks make the big spaces (Machine Hall, Cooling Towers,
  the Observation Ring, Conveyor Canyon, the Central Span, the boss arenas).

The world layout, chunk table and district briefs are below.

## Districts

### Power Distribution (chunks 34-40, manifest 19-24)

The deep plant's electrical heart, dead since the surge. A Dead Space style loop:
the hub shows three routes and one sealed gate (FEEDER 9 / MACHINE HALL / NO
SUPPLY). The player black-starts the plant: Substation A is dead and points to
Emergency Generation through the Cable Trench; starting the generators
(pwr_gen_online) brings the district's lights back on (switched lamps) and makes
Substation A and the Switch Hall live (state-gated electrical hazards appear on
floors that were safe before). The Switch Hall's catwalks then reach Load
Control, whose three-step load transfer (terminals with prerequisites) sets
pwr_feeder9: the hub gate opens and the mail relay wakes. One queued message
from LAB 4, stamped after the crash: "if you're alive don't come down to the
labs. go up." First high-voltage territory: hazard signage, arc flash
warnings, insulated mats, cages, bus ducts, cable trays everywhere. The
Cleanup Force swept this district: two dead technicians by the substation,
executed, not killed by creatures. Enemies: huntsmen in the trench (dark,
crouch), wasps in the switch hall, a two-man cleanup team at Load Control.

### Depthworks Medical (chunks 71-78, manifest 48 (subchapter))

The scariest part of the game. The clinic was never for injuries: it graded staff
by resonance exposure and moved high readers into observation. Two parts:
- The clinic front (Screening, Triage) is open early: LAB 4's line on the triage
  board: HELD FOR OBSERVATION / WARD C. The way into Ward C is sealed
  (med_override).
- The deep wing (Imaging, Pharmacy, Ward C, Surgery, Morgue) opens with the
  medical override disk found on the escort's body in Lab 4 (sets med_override;
  Lab 4's service door leads straight into Ward C). Ward C goes dark
  (med_blackout, set by a ScriptEvent when the player is deep in the ward):
  lamps on med_blackout invert switch off, a DarkZone jams the flashlight, and
  patients wake (Mutant spawns with wakeState med_blackout). The only light is
  the muzzle flash. Restoring the ward's power (a toggle terminal in Surgery)
  clears med_blackout. Her bed is empty, her band cut: SUBJECT LEFT WARD
  UNASSISTED / DOORS OPENED FROM INSIDE. The medical director's office in
  Surgery releases the morgue's quarantine door (med_morgue_release), the way on
  into Quarantine.
Use the imported med_ models (beds, monitors, carts, wheelchairs, x-ray boxes,
cabinets, autopsy table) and clinic tile surfaces. Flickering lamps,
dripping, breathing.
